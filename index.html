What is the game about?

Color Wheels Chronicles: The Quest for Color invites you to slow down and explore a living, breathing world built entirely out of color. You guide a small balloon-carrying heroine through the town of Prismoria, a place where every tile glows under your footsteps and every tower is made of stained glass.

The goal is simple and gentle: discover color, collect it, and make it your own. Chat with the quirky townsfolk, gather golden coins to level up, and style your character in outfits that each carry their own palette and personality.

Why you'll love it

🏰

A Town of Color

Stained-glass towers, a river that shifts hue, drifting lanterns, butterflies and petals. Prismoria always feels alive.

⛲

The Color-Wheel Fountain

At the center of town a glowing wheel turns above sparkling water, the heart of the world and its brightest landmark.

🧚

Meet the Prismorians

Wandering townsfolk with shimmering hair, glowing freckles and tiny crystals. Walk up and say hello, they wave back.

🪙

Coins & Levels

Gather golden coins scattered around town and fill your level bar. Each level-up is celebrated with a burst of light.

🎨

Deep Customization

Mix hairstyles, hair colors, outfits, hats, shoes, glasses and skin tones, or jump straight to a ready-made preset.

📱

Play Anywhere

Joystick and touch buttons on phones, keyboard on desktop. Jump, sprint, dance, sit down, or even take a nap.
